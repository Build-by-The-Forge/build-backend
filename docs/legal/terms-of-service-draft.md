## 2. What BUILD is

BUILD is a learning app for university students. It lets you set your academic context, keep and read study material, ask questions that are answered from your own material, practise, and take assessments your institution releases. **Some features described below are planned and are not yet available.** This document will be updated when they are.

## 6. Using BUILD

You agree to use BUILD for your own study and not to:
- break the law, or infringe anyone's rights;
- try to get into another person's account or data, or probe or disrupt the service;
- upload material that is unlawful, harmful, or that you have no right to use (section 9);
- use BUILD to cheat. Academic integrity rules of your institution apply to your use of BUILD. BUILD does not give AI help during an official assessment;
- reverse-engineer the app, or use bots or scrapers on it.

## 7. Assessments and results

- **Official assessments and results come from BUILD's records and your institution, not from AI.** A score is calculated by the system from your answers by fixed rules. **AI does not assign grades.** Release of results follows the institution's settings.
- Practice questions are separate from official assessments. Generated practice is labelled as generated and never counts as an official attempt or score.
- Official assessments need an internet connection. [In future, practice may work offline; any offline practice result is provisional until the server confirms it.]
- If you think a result is wrong, use the complaint route in section 15. [Counsel and the institution: define the academic appeal route and who decides.]

## 8. AI features

1. **BUILD uses AI to answer questions and draft explanations from your material.** AI can be wrong. Check important answers against your course material. BUILD shows the passages an answer came from, and says so when your material doesn't cover a question.
2. **No AI provider is currently approved to receive student material.** Until a provider has been reviewed and approved, BUILD does not send your questions or documents to any outside AI provider, and AI answers that would need one are unavailable. [When a provider is approved, this section will name it, say where it processes data, and describe what it receives. **A provider will not be listed before approval, and BUILD will not claim a use that the provider's own terms do not allow.**]
3. **Provider restrictions are real and some are open.** For example, one major provider's developer terms (the Gemini API Additional Terms, effective 2026-03-23, read on 2026-11-22 at https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms) say users must be 18 or older and that the service must not be used in an application directed to, or likely to be accessed by, people under 18, and state no educational exception. Whether and how BUILD's use fits those terms is **an open question**, and **no use will start until it is answered in writing by the provider or the arrangement is changed.** This does not describe any plan to avoid a provider's terms.
4. **Offline AI** on your device is planned for a later release and is not part of this version.
5. AI never makes decisions about you that have legal or similar significant effects, and never grades you.

That statement is your own assertion; BUILD does not check it legally and it is not legal clearance. Do not upload exam papers, textbooks or other material you may not copy unless you have permission.
